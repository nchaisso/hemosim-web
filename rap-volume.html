At the Bedside

RAP and volume status

The right atrial pressure is one of the most misread numbers in the ICU. As a rough guide, a RAP above about 10 mmHg points toward a cardiogenic picture, and one below about 5 mmHg toward hypovolemic or distributive shock. But a low RAP does not by itself mean a patient will respond to fluid, that is a different question, best answered by watching how the inferior vena cava and the stroke volume behave, not by the single number.

Read RAP in context. Positive end-expiratory pressure, obesity, pulmonary hypertension, and raised intra-abdominal pressure all lift the measured value without meaning the heart is full. This is why we treat RAP as one input into a volume-status assessment, alongside the IVC and dynamic measures, rather than a verdict on its own.
