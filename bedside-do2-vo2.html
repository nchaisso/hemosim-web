At the Bedside

Recognizing signs of a low DO2 / VO2 mismatch

Because DO2 and VO2 cannot be measured directly, we rely on surrogates:

  • B.U.S. organ perfusion: altered mentation, low urine output, delayed capillary refill and mottling.
  • Falling ScvO2 / SvO2 (a rising extraction ratio).
  • Rising arterial lactate.
  • A widening veno-arterial CO2 gap.

Each is imperfect and distorted by sedation, dialysis, warming blankets, and vasoactive drugs, so we corroborate several and reason about cardiac output.
